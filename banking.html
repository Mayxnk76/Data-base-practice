// Product

One ledger for accounts, cards, and goals

Everything money-related, reconciled automatically and shown the way you'd draw it on a whiteboard — not the way a bank's back office organizes it.

Real-time balance tracking

Every linked account syncs continuously, so the number on screen is the number in the bank — not yesterday's snapshot.

Cards, unified

Debit, credit, and prepaid — one card list with live limits, freeze controls, and per-card spend caps.

Scheduled transfers

Set recurring transfers once; Ledgerline handles retries and low-balance alerts for you.

Fraud watch

Every transaction scored on-device before it posts. Anomalies freeze the card, not just flag it.

Goal-based savings

Round-ups and auto-splits route spare change into named goals — visible progress, not vague totals.

// Security

Bank-grade, by construction

Security isn't a settings page here — it's the order operations happen in.

01

Connect

Link accounts through your bank's own secure login — Ledgerline never sees or stores your credentials.

02

Encrypt

Balances and transaction data are encrypted at rest and in transit with per-user keys.

03

Verify

Every session is device-bound and re-checked against behavioral signals, not just a password.

04

Alert

Unusual activity pauses the transaction and notifies you before money moves, not after.
